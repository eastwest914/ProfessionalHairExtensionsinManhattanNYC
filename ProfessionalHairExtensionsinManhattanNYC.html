Frequently Asked Questions

Which extension method is best for me?

It depends on your hair's thickness, your lifestyle and how you like to style it. Fine hair often suits tape-ins or hand-tied wefts, while others prefer keratin bonds or micro links. We will advise at your consultation.


Will extensions damage my natural hair?

When properly installed and maintained, extensions should not harm your hair. The method, placement and aftercare all matter, so we show you how to brush, wash and sleep with them.


Can you match my color exactly?

Yes. We blend extensions to your natural color and any highlights, and the colors can be customized for a seamless look.


What payment methods do you accept?

Hair Extension Services in Manhattan

Five professional application methods, plus custom color matching for length and volume.

Tape-In Hair Extensions

Tape-in extensions are made of thin wefts with a medical-grade adhesive strip. They lie flat against the head and are sandwiched between sections of your hair for a natural look.

They are quick to apply and comfortable to wear, and are a popular choice for adding volume or length to fine hair. They are repositioned periodically to keep them looking neat.

Keratin Bond Extensions

Keratin bond extensions are attached in small sections with a keratin tip that is heated and fused to your natural hair. The result is a flexible, natural feel.

Because each strand is placed separately, the style is easy to customize for volume or length, and the hair moves freely. A professional is needed for both installation and removal.

Call to Book

Micro Link Extensions

Micro link extensions use small beads to attach the hair to your own, without adhesive or heat. This makes them a gentle, versatile option for many hair types.

They can be tightened or moved up as your hair grows, and they work well for adding both length and body. Your stylist will place each link carefully to stay hidden.

Call to Book

Hand-Tied Extensions

Hand-tied wefts are sewn by hand into very thin tracks, which makes them lighter and flatter than machine-made wefts. They are a favorite for fine hair.

The ultra-thin track lies close to the scalp and can be hidden in styles from ponytails to loose waves. They are comfortable to wear and blend easily.

Call to Book

Weft Extensions

Weft extensions are sewn or bonded into the hair along a track, which makes them a good choice for adding a lot of fullness in a reasonable amount of time.

We select the weight and length based on your hair, then place the track at a comfortable level so it is hidden by your natural hair.
